# Pro Se Compass — Store Listing Copy

## Approved Product Position

Pro Se Compass is a **local-first case organization and legal-information research workspace** for people who are representing themselves. It helps users organize case records, retain source details, build a timeline, track expected materials, and capture research questions. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, does not represent users, and does not file or serve documents.

> Store copy must not promise outcomes, imply attorney representation, claim legal accuracy, authenticate evidence, or state that a document is ready to file.

## Google Play Copy

### Short description

Organize legal case records, sources, timelines, and discovery tasks.

### Full description

Pro Se Compass is a private, source-first workspace for organizing your own case materials. Capture records and their sources, track what still needs review, map a timeline with visible uncertainty, organize expected discovery materials, save public-source research notes, and keep source links alongside draft workspace materials.

Your workspace is local-first in this release. Pro Se Compass does not provide attorney representation, legal advice, legal conclusions, evidence authentication, filing, service, or outcome predictions. You are responsible for verifying records, current court rules, deadlines, and filing requirements. Before sharing or exporting material, remove unnecessary personal identifiers.

Use official and authorized sources where possible. The app labels user-provided accounts, unresolved questions, missing materials, potential discrepancies, and source status so that they are not presented as settled facts.

## Apple App Store Copy

### Subtitle

Organize your case materials

### Promotional text

Keep records, source details, timeline events, research leads, and unresolved questions organized in one local-first workspace.

### Description

Pro Se Compass helps self-represented people organize case materials for their own review. Build a source-linked record set, review an uncertainty-aware timeline, track expected evidence and discovery, capture public-source research notes, and preserve links between workspace drafts and the records that support them.

Pro Se Compass is an organization and research tool, not a law firm. It does not represent users, provide legal advice, determine legal strategy, authenticate evidence, promise outcomes, or file or serve documents. Users must independently verify records, court rules, deadlines, and filing requirements.

### App Review notes

No account or sign-in is required. The app opens to a scope disclosure and then demonstrates a fictional local workspace. All included example records are fictional. The app does not transmit case content to a server in this release.
