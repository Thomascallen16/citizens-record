# Ten Original Civic Narrations — Constitutional Principles

## Production Direction

Each narration is an original English-language civic recording presented in a serious, nonpartisan voice. The recordings are educational and source-linked. They are not legal advice, and they do not determine how constitutional language applies to an individual set of facts.

## Source Standard

The recordings quote or paraphrase constitutional language and pair each topic with the corresponding primary record. Interpretive lines are original narration rather than quoted constitutional text. The collection does not assert that a particular government action is unlawful or that a listener should take a particular legal action.
